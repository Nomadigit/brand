Logo

Waypoint

A lowercase n drawn as one route that ends in a red waypoint dot. It stands for moving and arriving.

Product icons

The master mark plus one cue for what the product does. The name next to it stays plain text.

Reminder · Nomadigit

Do

  • Use the mark alone at 16-48px (favicons, avatars, app icons); use the lockup when there is room for the name.
  • Keep clear space of at least half the mark's height on every side.
  • On photos or busy backgrounds place the inverse mark on a solid primary chip.
  • Use the mono variant when only one color is available (embossing, single-color print, system tray).

Don't

  • Don't recolor the route or the waypoint outside the palette, and don't swap their colors.
  • Don't stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows, gradients or glow.
  • Don't retype the wordmark in another font; always use the SVG.
  • Don't place the light mark on mid-tone backgrounds where contrast drops below 3:1.
  • Don't use the accent red for error states; errors use the darker semantic error color plus an icon and text.

Voice

Clear, friendly, concise, practical

  • Say what happens, then stop. One idea per sentence.
  • Talk to the reader as "ты" in Russian bots and products, "вы" in formal documents and CV.
  • Name things the way users call them, not the way the code calls them.
  • Errors explain what went wrong and what to do next, without apologies.
